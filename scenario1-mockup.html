Underlying row state

Two flags, two responsibilities — the previous owner's credit survives, the new owner has a clear queue.

Column Before After Why
is_done TRUE TRUE Preserved — Polat's completion credit survives the handover.
done_at 10 Eyl 14:32 10 Eyl 14:32 Preserved — the original completion timestamp stays put.
assigned_to u-polat u-neva Reassigned to Neva. The handover itself.
needs_redo FALSE TRUE New — flags "Neva owes an ack pass" without touching is_done.

Clicking the sky-500 "Yeniden Çalıştım" button calls POST /api/subtasks/:id/redo-ack, which clears needs_redo and writes a subtask_redo_acked history row.
